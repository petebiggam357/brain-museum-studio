              Brain Museum Studio · User Guide

              How to Use Everything

              Getting Started in 60 Seconds

              1. Connect your Muse 2: Power on the headband (white LEDs pulsing). Click Scan in the top bar, choose your Muse-XXXX device, and click Connect. The button turns green (Connected) once live packets flow.
              2. Check your Sensor Fit: Look at the four pills (TP9, AF7, AF8, TP10) on the Neural Waveform card. Green = good contact, Amber = fair, Red = adjust the headband behind your ears and across your forehead (a tiny drop of water on the forehead sensors helps!).
              3. Try Simulator Mode Anytime: Click Sim to test Calm, Focus, Drowsy, Blinks, Jaw Clenches, Neuro Lab, and Games without wearing the headset.
              4. Switch Themes: Click the Sun/Moon icon in the top-right to switch between Apple Obsidian Dark and Porcelain Light modes.

              Understanding Your Brainwaves

              Delta (1–4 Hz)

              Deep sleep, recovery, and slow-wave rest. High spikes while awake usually mean movement or blink artifacts.

              Theta (4–8 Hz)

              Deep meditation, creative daydreaming, drowsiness, and inward flow.

              Alpha (8–13 Hz)

              Calm, relaxed wakefulness. Try closing your eyes for 5 seconds — you’ll see your green Alpha bar jump!

              Beta (13–30 Hz)

              Active thinking, problem solving, and alert concentration. Drives your Focus ring.

              Gamma (30–44 Hz)

              High-level cognition & sensory binding (also sensitive to jaw/face muscle tension).

              Notch & Bandpass

              Keep Notch 60 Hz (US) or 50 Hz (EU) and 1–45 Hz Bandpass enabled to filter out wall-outlet hum and DC drift.

              Session Recorder & Markers

              Type a session name and click Record to save raw 256 Hz EEG to CSV + a JSON summary. Use Events & Markers to tag moments like "Eyes Closed" or "Math Problem".

              Neuro Lab — Neurofeedback, Mind Cursor & Virtual Keyboard

              1. Step 1 — Calibrate Baseline (10s): Sit still and relax normally, then click Calibrate Baseline (10s). The studio measures your personal resting Focus and sets a custom threshold above your baseline. Adjust the Focus Threshold slider anytime to make it easier or harder.
              2. Step 2 — Neural Levitation: Watch the glowing orb. Engage active mental focus (e.g., mental math, reading, or concentrating on a single point) to lift the orb above the dashed line into the green In The Zone area. Enable Harmonic Audio for gentle chimes when you hold focus.
              3. Step 3 — Hybrid Mind Cursor: Check Enable Cursor and gently turn/tilt your head to steer the blue cursor using the Muse 2 gyroscope. Aim at the orange target ring and trigger a click using Double Blink, Jaw Clench, or Focus Dwell (1s). Turn on Focus Clutch if you only want the cursor to move while your Focus is in the zone!
              4. Step 4 — Brainwave Virtual Keyboard: Choose your trigger (Focus Surge, Double Blink, or Jaw Clench), pick a scan speed, and click Start Scanner. First trigger locks the highlighted Row; second trigger types the highlighted Key. Click Speak to read your message aloud.
              5. Real OS Mouse / Typing (Optional): Everything starts in SAFE sandbox mode. To move your real Linux desktop cursor or type into other apps, arm OS Mouse or OS Type (requires ydotool on Wayland/COSMIC).

              Neuro Runner: Cortex Kingdom

              Guide Musey through Cortex Kingdom using your brainwaves and gestures! Click Start Game (or press Space).

              • Blink/Jaw Jump + Focus Glide (Default): Blink or clench your jaw to jump (and double-jump in mid-air!). Hold Focus above your threshold to activate Neural Glide (slow-fall) and an Orb Magnet Aura.
              • Pure Focus Jetpack: Raise Focus above threshold to fly upward through coin arcs; relax to descend smoothly.
              • Jaw Jump + Head-Tilt Speed: Clench jaw to jump and tilt your head left/right to slow down or speed up.
              • Scoring: Synapse Orbs = +10, Stomp a red Noise Glitch from above = +25, Alpha Star = +50 + 5.5s Shield, Focus Zone = 2× Multiplier.
              • Keyboard Fallback: Press Space to jump and hold Shift to simulate Focus Boost anytime.

              BCI Triggers & OSC Streaming

              BCI Controls Tab

              Create rules that map gestures (Double Blink, Jaw Clench, Head Tilt) to actions like Media Play/Pause, OSC Messages, Keystrokes, or HTTP Webhooks. Test any rule safely with the Test button. Nothing fires automatically until you click Arm Triggers.

              OSC Bridge Tab

              Click Start OSC Stream to broadcast live EEG, band powers, heart rate, IMU, gestures, and ML state over UDP (default 127.0.0.1:5000) into MuseLab, TouchDesigner, Ableton Live, Unity, or Godot.

              Training Your Personal Brain-State Classifier

              1. Collect State #1: Type a label like Eyes Closed, close your eyes for 3 seconds, and click Auto 5x to capture 5 EEG snapshots.
              2. Collect State #2: Type a second label like Deep Focus, do mental math or read intensely, and click Auto 5x again.
              3. Train Model: Click Train Personal Model. You’ll immediately see your Leave-One-Out accuracy score (e.g. 90%).
              4. Live Prediction: Your live predicted mental state now appears in the top header pill and broadcasts over OSC (/muse/ml/state)!

              Quick Troubleshooting

              • Muse 2 not showing in Scan: Make sure the headset isn’t already connected to your phone’s Muse app (Bluetooth LE only allows one active connection at a time), and confirm Linux Bluetooth is powered on.
              • Noisy / flatline channels: Tuck hair behind your ears, wipe the forehead sensors gently, and wait 3–5 seconds for the bandpass filter to settle.
              • Focus ring stuck high or low: Run Calibrate Baseline (10s) in Neuro Lab or slide the Focus Threshold to match your current resting level.
              • Wayland OS Mouse (`ydotool`): On Pop!_OS COSMIC / Wayland, install ydotool and make sure the ydotoold service is running if you arm real OS mouse/typing.
